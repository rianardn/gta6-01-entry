# Neon Harbor

An original open-world crime game set in **Port Meridian**, a sun-soaked harbour city. You are **Rae Calloway**, a courier with a stolen past, working jobs for fixer **Marisol Vega** while the Meridian PD — led by Sgt. Ilya Brandt — watches the streets.

## Play

- **Explore on foot and by car** — five districts (Downtown, Old Town, Marina, Foundry Row, Harbor Lights, Sandside beach), traffic that follows the street grid, pedestrians who stroll and flee.
- **Steal and drive** — walk up to any car and press **E**. Parked cars are free; jacking one with a driver, or lifting a police cruiser, brings heat.
- **Wanted level** — 0–5 stars. Cops chase on foot and in cruisers, ram your car, and bust you if they get close. Stay out of sight and the heat fades.
- **Five story missions** — First Fare, Hot Wheels, Street Heat, The Drop, and the finale, Harbor Heist — plus repeatable courier jobs and cash/health/ammo pickups.
- **Combat** — mouse aim, click to shoot, on-foot shootouts with the Tide gang.
- **HUD + minimap** — cash, reputation, health, wanted stars, clock, district name, mission objective, speed, and a live circular minimap with waypoint, cops and the player arrow.
- **Day/night cycle, save/load** — a full day runs 4 minutes; progress autosaves after every mission and can be saved from the pause menu (localStorage).
- **Sound** — fully synthesised WebAudio: engine hum, sirens, gunshots, pickups, mission jingles and a synthwave bassline. No audio files. **M** mutes.

## Controls

WASD move / drive · E enter or steal a car · Mouse aim, Click shoot · Shift sprint · Space handbrake · Esc pause & save · M mute

## Run it

Serve `game/` with any static server and open `index.html` in desktop Chrome. For local testing: `node serve.ts` then http://localhost:8080.

Screenshots are staged with `?shot=start|drive|chase|mission` (deterministic renders of the game itself).

## Originality

Everything — the city, characters, vehicles, story, art and music — is original fiction made for this game. Nothing from any commercial game appears in it.
